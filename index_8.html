The Lounge

Your local indoor golf experience

The Fairway at 105 brings the golf-course experience closer to home. Whether you want to practise your game, compete against friends, or enjoy a relaxed round, the simulator gives you the chance to play all year, regardless of weather.

Every booking is a private session in the bay of your choice, 1 to 4 hours, with full player and shot data tracked as you go.

  • A private simulator session, 1 to 4 hours
  • Access to eight virtual golf courses
  • Space for one to four players
  • Player and shot-performance tracking
  • Entry into our monthly competitions

Before You Arrive

Equipment guidelines

  • Please bring your own equipment (clubs, balls, and gear).
  • Ensure all clubs and shoes are clean before entering the simulator bay.
  • We highly recommend players wear golf gloves for optimal grip and performance.
  • Only clean, unmarked golf balls may be used to protect the impact screen.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long is each booking?

You choose — sessions run 1, 2, 3 or 4 hours, whatever suits your group.

How many people can play?

Bookings are available for one to four players.

How many courses are available?

Players can choose from eight virtual golf courses.

Is the price charged per session or per person?

Prices are a flat rate per group session, based on your number of players and session length — not per person. The total is calculated for you automatically.

What is included in the booking?

The booking includes simulator time for your selected session length.

Can I enter a monthly competition?

Yes — eligible players can enter the Nearest to the Pin competition.

Where is The Fairway at 105 located?

105 Chaucer, Orkney, North West.
